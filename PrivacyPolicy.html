K-Drama Factory Privacy Policy

最后更新日期 / Last Updated: 2026-08-27

中文

感谢您使用「韩剧工厂 / K-Drama Factory」。本应用不收集、不存储任何用户个人信息。本政策说明我们在使用第三方公开数据源、本地功能以及可选人工智能特性时的隐私实践。

English

Thank you for using "K-Drama Factory". This app does not collect or store any personal information. This policy explains our privacy practices when using public third-party data sources, on-device features, and optional AI services.

1. 我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

中文

  • 应用不要求注册、登录或提供任何个人身份信息。
  • 为展示韩剧资讯，应用会向 TVMaze 公开 REST API（https://api.tvmaze.com）发起网络请求，获取公开可用的剧集条目、海报图、简介、演员表、播出季次与评分。当您使用搜索功能时，搜索关键词会发送至 TVMaze 服务器以完成检索。
  • 应用使用 UserDefaults 仅存储本地设置：界面语言（中文/英文）、追剧清单（watchlist）与观看进度、AI 对话历史缓存、应用打开次数（用于好评弹窗逻辑）以及首次打开时间，均不离开您的设备。
  • 用户点击「联系开发者/反馈」时，应用会调用系统邮件客户端（mailto）发送邮件；邮件内容由用户主动填写，不会自动上传任何信息。

English

  • No registration, login, or personal identification is required.
  • To display Korean drama information, the app sends network requests to the TVMaze public REST API (https://api.tvmaze.com) to fetch publicly available drama entries, poster images, synopses, cast, season schedules, and ratings. When you use search, the search keywords are sent to TVMaze's server to perform lookups.
  • The app uses UserDefaults only for local settings: interface language (Chinese/English), watchlist (tracking list) and viewing progress, AI conversation history cache, app launch count (used for review prompt logic), and first-open date. None of this leaves your device.
  • Tapping "Contact Developer / Feedback" opens the system Mail app (mailto) for you to compose a message; no information is automatically uploaded.

2. 人工智能（AI）功能 / AI Features

中文

  • 「AI 搜同款」与「AI 剧情解读」功能，会将被查询的韩剧名称、类型、简介等 文本内容 发送至第三方大语言模型接口（OpenAI 兼容协议）以生成推荐与解读。我们 不会 随请求发送您的账号、设备标识符（IDFA/IDFV）、位置信息或任何个人身份信息，也不会将这些内容用于训练或留存。
  • 上述请求由您每次主动触发（如点击「问 AI」按钮）才会发出；应用不会在后台自动调用 AI 接口，也不会收集任何行为日志。

English

  • The "AI Match" and "AI Plot" features send the text of the queried drama's title, genres, and synopsis to a third-party large-language-model API (OpenAI-compatible protocol) to generate recommendations and analysis. We do not send any account, device identifier (IDFA/IDFV), location data, or personal information with the request, and we do not use the content for training or retention.
  • The above AI requests are only triggered on your explicit action (e.g. tapping the "Ask AI" button); the app does not call the AI API in the background and does not collect any behavioral logs.

3. 数据共享 / Data Sharing

中文

我们不收集、不出售、不共享任何个人信息。除上文明确披露的第三方 API 调用外，您的数据不会离开您的设备。

English

We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information. Beyond the third-party API calls explicitly disclosed above, your data does not leave your device.

4. 第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

中文

  • TVMaze：剧集数据、海报图与公开元数据。搜索关键词会上传至 TVMaze 以完成检索。
  • 第三方大语言模型 API（AI 搜同款 / AI 剧情解读 / 智能问答）：仅发送查询文本；不会附带账号、设备标识或定位信息。
  • Apple UserNotifications：本地通知（追剧更新提醒）。通知完全在您的设备上编排。
  • Apple SKAdNetwork / StoreKit 评分弹窗：触发系统级应用评分弹窗；该接口不会向我们返回设备信息。
  • 系统邮件客户端：用户主动反馈。

English

  • TVMaze: drama data, poster images and public metadata. Search keywords are sent to TVMaze to perform lookups.
  • Third-party LLM API (AI Match / AI Plot / Q&A): only query text is sent; no account, device identifier, or location data is attached.
  • Apple UserNotifications: local notifications (episodes reminder). Notifications are scheduled entirely on your device.
  • Apple SKAdNetwork / StoreKit review prompt: triggers the system-level in-app review prompt. The interface never returns device information to us.
  • System Mail app: user-initiated feedback.

5. 本地通知 / Local Notifications

中文

当您将某部「在看」的连载韩剧加入追剧清单后，应用会在该剧更新时通过 设备本地通知 提醒您。通知完全在设备本地编排，不经过任何服务器。

English

When you add an airing drama you are "watching", the app reminds you via on-device local notifications when a new episode is released. Notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and never go through a server.

6. 未成年人 / Children

中文

本应用不会收集任何用户的个人信息，亦不会主动识别用户年龄。所有设置、追剧清单与观看进度均仅保存在您的设备本地，13 岁以下儿童也可在家长指导下安全使用。

English

This app does not collect any personal information from any user and does not attempt to identify a user's age. All settings, the watchlist, and viewing progress are stored only on your device. Children under 13 may use the app safely under parental guidance.
