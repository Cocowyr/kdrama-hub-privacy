隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月

1. 我们不收集你的个人信息

韩剧迷（K-Drama Hub）在设计上不收集、不存储任何可识别个人身份的信息。你在本应用内的追剧清单、观看进度、语言偏好等均仅保存在你的设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器。

2. 我们不使用广告追踪

本应用不展示任何广告，也不请求广告标识符（IDFA）或使用任何用户行为追踪。应用中集成的第三方广告 SDK 处于未初始化、未启用的休眠状态，绝不会加载广告或追踪你的行为。

3. 人工智能（AI）功能

「AI 搜同款」与「AI 剧情解读」功能会将被查询的韩剧名称、类型、简介等文本内容发送给第三方大语言模型接口以生成推荐与解读。我们不会随请求发送账号、设备标识或任何个人身份信息，也不会将这些内容用于训练或留存。

4. 本地通知

当你将某部「在看」的连载韩剧加入追剧清单后，应用会在该剧更新时通过设备本地通知提醒你。通知完全在设备本地编排，不经过任何服务器。

5. 订阅

会员订阅通过 Apple App Store 完成，相关支付信息与账户由 Apple 管理，我们不会接触到你的付款凭证。

6. 联系我们

如有任何隐私相关疑问，可邮件联系：1046850694@qq.com

本政策仅适用于韩剧迷应用本身。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: August 2026

1. We do not collect your personal information

K-Drama Hub is designed so that it does not collect or store any personally identifiable information. Your watchlist, viewing progress, and language preference are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

2. We do not use advertising tracking

The app does not display any ads and does not request the advertising identifier (IDFA) or use any behavioral tracking. Any third-party ad SDK included in the app remains uninitialized and dormant, and never loads ads or tracks you.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) features

The "AI Match" and "AI Plot" features send the text of the queried drama's title, genres, and synopsis to a third-party large-language-model API to generate recommendations and analysis. We do not send any account, device identifier, or personal information with the request, and we do not use the content for training or retention.

4. Local notifications

When you add an airing drama you are "watching", the app reminds you via on-device local notifications when a new episode is released. Notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and never go through a server.

5. Subscriptions

Membership subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store; payment and account information is managed by Apple, and we never receive your payment credentials.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions, email: 1046850694@qq.com

This policy applies solely to the K-Drama Hub app.
